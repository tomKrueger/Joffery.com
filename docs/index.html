The sprawl tax

If this feels familiar, you are who we built Joffery for

Growing companies outgrow duct tape. The pain is not laziness — it is too many systems with no shared home.

Renewals hiding in the statement

SaaS subscriptions and contract renewals live in credit card exports and a spreadsheet someone updated last quarter — until auto-renew hits.

Monday’s new hire

Onboarding is copied from three different docs. Nobody knows which version is current or whether IT, HR, and the manager are aligned.

Five places for “my tasks”

Jira, ServiceNow, Smartsheet columns, spreadsheets, and personal to-do lists — with no single answer to “what do I actually need to do today?”

Strategy in a deck

Quarterly priorities, weekly scorecards, and company direction sit in a slide deck nobody opens after week two.

Reviews & risk on enterprise terms

Performance reviews, role-based training, and risk tracking either do not happen or require tools priced for much larger companies.

Compliance beside you, not against you

If you use Vanta or Drata, they should stay your system of record. Joffery reads from them — it does not ask you to rip and replace.

Platform modules

Five pillars, one operational home

01

My Work

Personal task command center pulling assignments from Jira, ServiceNow, Smartsheet project sheets, and spreadsheets — grouped by source, project, or due date, plus a home for orphan tasks.

02

Tools, Spend & Access

Every SaaS tool you pay for, who has a seat, unused-seat waste, monthly and annual burn, and upcoming contract renewals before auto-renew hits.

Layered Onboarding & Role Learning

Company-wide day-by-day base plan plus department add-ons with per-hire progress, paired with a learning catalog mapped to roles, tools, and products you sell.

Goals, Scorecards & Reviews

A one-page company plan, 90-day priorities, and a weekly team scorecard that flow into lightweight employee performance reviews.

05

Risk Register & Compliance Companion

Simple risk and vendor views that work alongside Vanta and Drata — read-only sync when you have them; local register until you are ready.
